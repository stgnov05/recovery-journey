Recovery Resources & Support

There is more than one path toward recovery. Explore peer support, faith-based communities, practical tools, family support, and treatment options.

Find a Recovery Community

Different approaches work for different people. These independent organizations offer meetings, practical tools, or spiritual support. You can explore more than one.

Alcoholics Anonymous (AA)

A Twelve Step fellowship for people who want to stop drinking, centered on mutual support and shared experience.

Find AA meetings

Narcotics Anonymous (NA)

A Twelve Step fellowship for people seeking recovery from drug addiction, with in-person and virtual meetings.

Find NA meetings

SMART Recovery

A secular, science-informed approach that uses practical tools for managing urges, motivation, thoughts, and behavior. Meetings are available online and in person.

Explore SMART Recovery

Celebrate Recovery

A Christian, Christ-centered recovery ministry offering community and support for people facing hurts, habits, and hang-ups.

Explore Celebrate Recovery

Support for Families & Friends

Al-Anon Family Groups

Support for people whose lives have been affected by someone else's drinking. Al-Anon focuses on the wellbeing of families and friends.

Find Al-Anon meetings

Nar-Anon Family Groups

A peer-support fellowship for relatives and friends affected by someone else's addiction.

Explore Nar-Anon
