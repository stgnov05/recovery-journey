🧭 When a Moment Feels Difficult

Try this simple grounding exercise to reconnect with the present.

  1. Name 5 things you can see.
  2. Notice 4 things you can feel.
  3. Listen for 3 things you can hear.
  4. Notice 2 things you can smell.
  5. Notice 1 thing you can taste.

Then consider one small, safe next step: drink water, take a short walk, contact a supportive person, or attend a meeting.
