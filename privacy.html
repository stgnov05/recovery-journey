Your Privacy Matters

How Recovery Journey handles visitor information, external links, and optional personal recovery tools.

Last updated: October 2026. Recovery Journey is a personal website maintained by Steven Gregory.

Information you choose to share

Browsing this site does not require you to create an account. If you contact the site or send a donation, information you share is handled by the service you use (such as your email provider, Venmo, or Cash App) under its own privacy policy.

Interactive tools

Some recovery tools may save entries in your browser's local storage. This information is generally stored on your device rather than sent to the website owner. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to view it. Clearing browser data can erase it. Do not enter information you would not want stored on that device.

Hosting and third-party services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and other infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs according to their own policies. External links, including meeting finders and payment services, have their own privacy practices.

Analytics and cookies

No site-owner analytics or advertising tracking is intentionally installed in these new pages. This statement should be reviewed if analytics, advertising, embedded widgets, or other third-party scripts are added later.

Contact

For questions about this page, visit the contact page. Please avoid sharing sensitive health information through public channels.

This is a practical website disclosure, not a legal guarantee of privacy.
