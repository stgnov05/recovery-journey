Living in Recovery

Recovery is more than a milestone. It's the life we keep building, one day at a time.

For every season of recovery

Whether you're counting your first days or reflecting on decades of progress, you still deserve connection, encouragement, and room to grow. There is no single timeline for healing.

🌱 Early recovery

Build a realistic daily routine, identify safe support people, and make a plan for difficult moments.

Begin here

🤝 Building stability

Explore work, housing, finances, boundaries, relationships, and the habits that support recovery.

Explore tools

🌊 Long-term recovery

Stay connected, revisit your goals, mentor when appropriate, and make space for purpose, joy, and new experiences.

Read reflections

Practices worth revisiting

Connection over isolation

Keep in touch with people who respect your recovery. A meeting, a call, or an honest conversation can matter.

Care for the whole person

Sleep, nourishing food, movement, and appropriate healthcare support your overall well-being.

Purpose beyond sobriety

Learning, service, creativity, faith, nature, and meaningful work can help life feel fuller.

Adjust when life changes

Transitions, grief, success, and stress can all affect recovery. Update your support plan when needed.

Need more support?

You never have to wait until things become overwhelming. Explore meetings, treatment resources, and recovery housing.
