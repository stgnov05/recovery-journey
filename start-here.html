A welcoming first step toward addiction recovery, with meeting finders, practical suggestions, and encouragement.

Start with one small step. You don't have to solve everything today. If you're in immediate danger, call 911. In the U.S., call or text 988 for crisis support.

Where to begin

1. Connect with someone

Reach out to a trusted person, recovery support group, or healthcare professional. You deserve support.

2. Find a meeting

Meet others who understand the journey. You can attend to listen before deciding what comes next.

Find AA meetings

Find NA meetings

3. Make a plan for today

Choose one achievable action: drink water, eat, rest, call someone, or make it to a meeting.

Need professional help?

In the U.S., FindTreatment.gov helps locate treatment services. The SAMHSA National Helpline is 1-800-662-HELP (4357).

Alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous. If you drink heavily or regularly, seek medical advice before stopping suddenly; withdrawal may require supervised treatment.

Encouragement for today

Recovery can begin with a single decision to ask for help. Faith may be a source of strength, and support is available whether or not you share the site's religious beliefs.
