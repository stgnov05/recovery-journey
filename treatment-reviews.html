Review submissions are not open yet. Never post another person's private medical information.

What you'll be able to explore

Search by treatment center name and location, and compare the types of care offered, such as residential treatment, outpatient services, and medication treatment.

Read firsthand accounts describing communication, respect, safety, aftercare planning, and the overall experience. Ratings will reflect individual opinions, not clinical quality scores.

What you'll be able to share

Visitors will be able to identify a facility, select an approximate treatment period, choose an overall rating, and write about what helped or could have been better.

Reviews will be screened before publication. We will ask contributors not to name other patients or disclose private health details about anyone else.

Safety and fairness

Reviews will be labeled as personal experiences. We will prohibit threats, hate speech, fabricated accounts, promotional spam, and identifying information about other patients.

Facilities will have a way to flag factual concerns without being able to remove honest criticism simply because it is negative.

Privacy comes first

We plan to offer public display names rather than requiring contributors to publish their legal names. Submission details and any account information will be handled separately from public reviews.

Before collecting information, we will publish clear consent, retention, deletion, and moderation policies.

Need treatment now? In the U.S., visit FindTreatment.gov to search for treatment providers. For confidential support and referrals, see SAMHSA's National Helpline. For immediate danger, call 911.
