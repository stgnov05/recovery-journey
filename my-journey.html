My Recovery Journey

My past is part of my story. It does not have to be the end of it.

Why I'm Beginning Again

My name is Steven. For 34 years, alcohol was part of my life. Over time, addiction cost me opportunities, stability, and many of the things I hoped my life would become.

There have been difficult days when I felt lost, angry about the past, and uncertain about the future. I'm not sharing that to ask for pity. I'm sharing it because I know other people have felt that way, too.

Today, I'm choosing recovery. I know that rebuilding a life takes time, effort, support, and honesty. I don't have all the answers, and I won't pretend that I do.

I don't want addiction to write the rest of my story.

My faith in God is an important part of this journey. I've prayed through hope and through frustration, asking for strength, direction, peace, and a reason to look forward to tomorrow.

Joel 2:25 speaks of restoring lost years. I cannot undo the past, but I can make different choices today and work toward a better future.

That's why I started this website: to share what I'm learning, to be honest about the struggles, and to remind someone else that they don't have to face recovery alone.

One day at a time. One step toward a new life.

What I'm Working Toward

🌱 Lasting Recovery

Building healthy routines, seeking support, and making the next right choice, even when it's hard.

🤝 Connection & Community

Growing meaningful friendships, finding accountability, and encouraging others on their journeys.

☀️ Faith & Purpose

Trusting God as I rebuild, discovering new opportunities, and learning to live with hope.

There's Still More to the Story

Recovery isn't about having a perfect past or a perfect tomorrow. It's about taking an honest step forward today.
