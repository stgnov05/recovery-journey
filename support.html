Every Act of Kindness Matters

I created My Recovery Journey to share my experiences with addiction, faith, and the hope of beginning again.

As I work toward a more stable future, I’m also building this website so that others can find encouragement, practical recovery tools, and trustworthy resources. If my story or this site has been meaningful to you, and you're in a position to help, I would be deeply grateful for your support.

There is absolutely no obligation to give. Reading, sharing, and encouraging someone else on their journey are meaningful ways to support this mission too.

Important Donation Information

These are personal contributions to Steven Gregory. My Recovery Journey is a personal website, not a registered nonprofit or tax-exempt charity. Contributions are not tax-deductible charitable donations.

Funds go to Steven personally and may be used for his recovery-related living needs as well as the website purposes described above. Contributions are voluntary and do not purchase goods, services, or special access. Payments are handled by Venmo or Cash App under their terms; My Recovery Journey does not process or store payment information.

Please give only if doing so is comfortable for you. Never put your own financial well-being at risk to contribute.
