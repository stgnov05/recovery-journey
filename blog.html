A New Beginning: Choosing Recovery and Trusting God

By Steven Gregory · October 29, 2026

For 34 years, alcohol was a part of my life. Over those years, addiction took more from me than I ever imagined it could. I lost opportunities, stability, relationships, and pieces of the future I once dreamed of having.

There have been moments when I've looked at my life and wondered how I could possibly begin again. I've felt angry about the years I've lost, lonely, uncertain, and afraid of what comes next.

But somewhere in the middle of all that pain, I made a decision.

I don't want addiction to write the rest of my story.

Choosing recovery doesn't mean everything suddenly becomes easy. I still have things to rebuild, mistakes to learn from, and challenges ahead of me. But I'm beginning to understand that I don't have to fix my entire life overnight.

I only have to take the next right step.

And through this journey, I'm holding on to my faith in God.

I've prayed when I felt hopeful, and I've prayed when I felt angry and discouraged. I've asked God for peace, strength, direction, and a reason to look forward to tomorrow.

I don't have every answer. I don't know exactly what my future will look like. But I believe God can still bring purpose and meaning into a life that has been through so much.

One passage that speaks to my heart is Joel 2:25, where God speaks of restoring the years that have been lost.

I cannot go back and reclaim the past. But I can begin building something different today.

That's why I created Recovery Journey.

I want this website to be a place where I can share my progress, my struggles, my faith, and the lessons I learn along the way. And if my experiences help even one person feel less alone, then something meaningful can come from what I've been through.

I'm not claiming to have everything figured out. I'm simply a man who has decided to keep trying, keep believing, and keep moving forward.

My past is real. My recovery is real. And my future is still unwritten.

One day at a time. One step toward a new life.

— Steven Gregory

By Steven Gregory · Recovery Journey

Welcome to my Recovery Journal.

This is a place to reflect on what it means to rebuild a life—one decision, one day, and one step at a time. Recovery is not a straight line, and it doesn't require pretending the past never happened.

Some days may bring hope and energy. Others may bring uncertainty. But each day offers an opportunity to keep going, reach out for support, and choose a different path.

My story isn't finished. And neither is yours.

My hope is that this journal becomes a place for honesty, encouragement, faith, and connection. If you're walking your own recovery journey, you're welcome here.
