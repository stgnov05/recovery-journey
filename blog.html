Every New Beginning Starts Somewhere

By Stephen Thomas Gregory · Recovery Journey

Welcome to my Recovery Journal.

This is a place to reflect on what it means to rebuild a life—one decision, one day, and one step at a time. Recovery is not a straight line, and it doesn't require pretending the past never happened.

Some days may bring hope and energy. Others may bring uncertainty. But each day offers an opportunity to keep going, reach out for support, and choose a different path.

My story isn't finished. And neither is yours.

My hope is that this journal becomes a place for honesty, encouragement, faith, and connection. If you're walking your own recovery journey, you're welcome here.

— Stephen Thomas Gregory

More reflections to come. Future entries can explore recovery milestones, spiritual reflections, lessons learned, and practical encouragement. This first entry is a suggested introduction that Stephen can revise before sharing.
